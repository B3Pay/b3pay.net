B3Forge runs at [forge.b3pay.net](https://forge.b3pay.net). You can look around without an account, and you sign in when you want to build.

## Without signing in

- Browse the Community's workflows, and open a shared workflow's page.
- [Explore canisters](canisters.mdx): read a canister's methods and call one.
- Use the Candid playground.

## Sign in

B3Forge signs you in with [Internet Identity](https://id.ai): no password and no seed phrase. Your B3Forge account is tied to that identity.

The first time you sign in, B3Forge asks for a display name and an email address to create your account and activate Forge AI. Your display name appears on the workflows you publish. Your email is used for notifications and account recovery, and is never shown publicly. You can change both later in **Settings → Profile**.

With an account you can:

- ask Forge to build workflows ([Build with Forge](build-with-forge.mdx));
- open the studio and save workflows to your library ([Edit in the studio](studio.mdx));
- publish, favorite and fork workflows ([Save, share and fork](share.mdx)).

## Forge AI's daily limit

Each account can make a limited number of Forge AI requests a day. **Settings → Forge AI** shows how much of today's limit is left, when it resets, and how a request travels from your browser to the model.

## The command box

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, anywhere in B3Forge. One box hands a prompt to Forge, opens a canister by its ID, searches the catalogs, or goes to a page: the studio, your library, the Community, the canister explorer, the playground or your settings.

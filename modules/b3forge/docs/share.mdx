## Your library

**My library**, in the account menu, lists the workflows you saved, in three views: **Drafts**, **Published** and **Favorites**. You can search them and filter them by label. A workflow you save starts as a draft, and B3Forge shows a draft only to you.

## A workflow's settings

**Workflow settings**, in the studio or on a workflow's card in your library, has four tabs:

- **Preview** runs the saved workflow, the same way its run page does.
- **General** holds the name, the description and the labels. Labels are used in search and to group workflows in the Community. **AI Fill** writes all three from the graph.
- **Sharing & Access** publishes the workflow and sets who can run it.
- **Danger Zone** deletes it.

## Publish to the Community

In **Sharing & Access**, turn on **Community visibility**. The workflow has to be saved from the studio first. Once it is published:

- the Community lists it, with your display name;
- it has a run page: **Copy Share Link** copies its address, and **Open Public Page** opens it.

Turn **Community visibility** off to make it a draft again. Its run page and share link stop working.

**Require login to run** asks every visitor to sign in before they can run the workflow, so its calls are made with their own identity, never anonymously.

<Aside type="caution" title="Published means readable">
  Anyone can open a published workflow and read every step of it, with or without **Require login to run**. Keep
  secrets out of workflows.
</Aside>

## Other people's workflows

The **Community** lists every published workflow. Search it by name, filter it by label, and press **Load more** for more.

On a workflow's run page or its card:

- **Fork** makes your own private copy and opens it in the studio. Its **New Name** starts as "Fork of …", and you can change it. Forking needs a B3Forge account.
- **Remix with Forge** asks Forge for a variation of the workflow (see [Build with Forge](build-with-forge.mdx)).
- The star adds it to your favorites. In the Community, the **Favorites** filter shows the ones you starred.

On your own workflow's run page, **Edit in studio** takes the place of **Fork**.

## Delete a workflow

**Danger Zone → Delete this workflow** removes the workflow and its saved document for good. It can't be undone.

B3Forge builds workflows of Internet Computer canister calls. You say what should happen in your own words. Forge reads each canister's Candid interface, wires the calls into a typed workflow and checks every binding between them. You review the workflow in the studio, then run it in your browser with your Internet Identity. B3Forge is in beta.

<LinkButton href="https://forge.b3pay.net" icon="external">Open B3Forge</LinkButton>

## How it works

1. **You ask.** Describe the outcome you want. The studio opens with your prompt filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send.
2. **Forge plans.** It searches its canister catalog, fetches the Candid interfaces it needs, and lays out nodes with typed bindings between them.
3. **The types are checked.** The engine that runs workflows, written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly, checks every binding. When the check fails, Forge revises the graph and checks it again.
4. **You review it and run it.** The draft opens in the studio. The workflow runs in your browser and signs with your identity, and each update call shows you its exact arguments and waits for your approval before it is sent.

No B3Pay server runs your workflows. B3Forge's own canister stores them and lists the ones you share. What you ask Forge goes to B3Pay's AI service, which counts it against a daily limit.

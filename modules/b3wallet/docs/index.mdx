B3Wallet is a wallet that lives in a canister on the Internet Computer. One canister holds accounts on Bitcoin, Ethereum and the Internet Computer, and can have one owner or several. There is no registration and no backup phrase: whoever controls the canister controls its funds.

<Aside type="danger" title="Do not hold funds in it">
  B3Wallet's code was last changed in March 2024. Its approvals and its custody have gaps that are not fixed. It is here
  for its code and its design, not for real funds.
</Aside>

## Owners and signers

- **One owner**: the `b3wallet_basic` canister.
- **Several signers**: the `b3wallet` canister. Each signer has a role, and a signer can request an operation, such as a transfer or a new signer, for the others to approve. A requested operation runs once more than half of the signers allowed to approve it confirm it. Not every method of the canister goes through these approvals.

## Accounts

A wallet starts with one account and can add more. Every account is derived from the same canister, at a different index.

## Upgrades

The canister can upgrade itself, so a wallet can take new versions without being recreated.

## Building your own wallet

Three Rust libraries in the repository's `backend/lib` folder are for building other wallets:

- **`b3_utils`**: the foundations every wallet needs.
- **`operations`**: authorization and permissions for each wallet operation.
- **`b3wallet_lib`**: accounts, keys, balances and transfers across chains and tokens.

## Running it locally

The repository's README covers starting a local replica with `dfx`, with Bitcoin support optional, and deploying the canisters: see [B3Pay/B3Wallet](https://github.com/B3Pay/B3Wallet#setting-up-and-running-b3wallet-locally).

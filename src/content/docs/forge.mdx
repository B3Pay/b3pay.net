## Today: in beta on the Internet Computer

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A visual graph" icon="puzzle">
    Place canister calls as nodes and bind one node's output to the next node's input. The bindings are type-checked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expressions" icon="pencil">
    Refer to an earlier step's output in an expression, such as `$N0.amount`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Runs in your browser" icon="laptop">
    Workflows run in the browser with a delegated identity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A community catalog" icon="open-book">
    Publish your workflows, and fork other people's.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Next: B3Forge for EVM

<p class="status-line">In development</p>

Workflows built from any contract with a verified ABI, where each step that writes is simulated, decoded and shown with its ERC-7730 descriptor before you confirm it. Want it for your team? [Tell us what you would automate](/contact/?topic=b3forge).
